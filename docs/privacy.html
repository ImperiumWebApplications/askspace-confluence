Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Product: AskSpace - Questions & FAQ for Confluence

1. Zero External Data Egress ("Runs on Atlassian")

AskSpace is built 100% on Atlassian Forge and Atlassian-hosted Forge SQL storage. AskSpace operates no external servers, transmits no End-User Data outside Atlassian Cloud, and uses zero third-party data subprocessors. All app compute and data remain strictly inside Atlassian's isolated cloud boundary and follow your Confluence Cloud site's Data Residency region.

2. Data Processed Inside Atlassian Forge

To deliver internal Q&A functionality inside your Confluence tenant, AskSpace stores only data explicitly created by users within the app inside Atlassian-hosted Forge SQL:

  • Questions, answers, comments, tags, votes, and watch subscriptions created inside AskSpace.
  • Atlassian Account ID and public display name of the user who authored, answered, commented on, or verified a thread (fetched at runtime via Confluence REST API).
  • Audit log entries recording Q&A lifecycle events (creation, acceptance, tag merges, page promotions) for customer governance.

3. Data Retention & Deletion (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP Rights)

  • In-App Deletion: Authors and administrators may delete individual questions or answers at any time; deleting a question immediately cascades and deletes all associated answers, comments, votes, watchers, and notifications.
  • Uninstall Cleanup: When a Confluence site administrator uninstalls AskSpace, Atlassian automatically schedules removal of the app's tenant-isolated Forge storage in accordance with Atlassian Forge lifecycle policies.
  • Data Principal / Subject Requests: Because AskSpace does not store customer data outside your Atlassian Cloud instance, site administrators maintain direct control. For any privacy inquiry, contact our support team via the Support Portal.

4. Cookies & Analytics

AskSpace sets no third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or external analytics beacons.
