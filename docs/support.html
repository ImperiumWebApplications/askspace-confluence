Documentation & Customer Support

Quick Start (Under 2 Minutes)

  1. Open Company-Wide Q&A: Click Apps → AskSpace Q&A in the top Confluence navigation bar.
  2. Open Space-Scoped Q&A: Open any Confluence Space and click AskSpace Q&A in the left Space sidebar.
  3. Embed Live FAQs on Any Page: In the Confluence editor, type /AskSpace to insert the AskSpace Q&A & FAQ macro. Toggle Show Verified Accepted Answers Inline to display both questions and verified answers directly on your page.
  4. Verify & Promote Answers: Either the question author or any Space/Site Admin can click Mark Accepted Solution, then click Publish to Confluence Page to generate a permanent formatted FAQ doc.
  5. Ask Atlassian Rovo: Invoke the AskSpace Q&A Assistant agent in Rovo to search verified company Q&A answers.
